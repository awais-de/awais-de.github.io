Comparative study of how political actors discuss sustainability on YouTube across six countries — USA, UK, Canada, Australia, India, and Pakistan.

Video links, comments, and engagement statistics were collected via the **YouTube Data API v3**, structured with **Pandas**, and scored with **VADER** sentiment analysis. Keyword frequency, temporal trends, and cross-country comparisons were then evaluated against engagement metrics.

**Findings:** "Climate Emergency" is the most-used keyword since 2019; "Renewable Energy Green Jobs" drew the highest view counts; comments on "Reforestation" content skewed most positive.

**Stack:** Python, YouTube Data API v3, Pandas, VADER
