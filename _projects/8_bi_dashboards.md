Four Power BI dashboards, each built around a different operational domain, sharing a common approach: import, model, and visualize an operational dataset into decision-ready KPIs.

- **Sales Analysis** — revenue, customer segmentation, shipment performance, returns, and yearly sales/profit trends.
- **Order Overview Analysis** — order volume, units dispatched, production breakdown, and inquiry status.
- **Warehouse Analysis** — inventory levels, delivery-method performance, and return-reason breakdowns.
- **Mobile Network (Telecom) Analysis** — call/SMS/data usage, device popularity, and geographic call distribution.

**Stack:** Power BI, DAX

**Status:** Complete

**Code:** [SalesAnalysis](https://github.com/awais-de/SalesAnalysis) · [OrderOverviewAnalysis](https://github.com/awais-de/OrderOverviewAnalysis) · [WarehouseAnalysis](https://github.com/awais-de/WarehouseAnalysis) · [TelecomAnalysis](https://github.com/awais-de/TelecomAnalysis)
